General

Most rules are common sense and it would therefore be impossible to list all the things you could to to get yourself banned. This page is not acting as a complete list of bannable offenses - it instead aims to clear up some confusion about those rules that may not be obvious.

As a general rule if you wouldn't do it in real life don't do it on the forums.

Section Rules

Each section/sub-forum has its own rules, which you can find in sticky threads ( Yellow threads on top of each section/sub-forum ).

Violating the section rules will also get you banned.

Advertising

If you're advertising something of yours (websites, videos, servers, etc) shortly after registering or with a small post count, you're probably going to get permabanned. We don't want advertising gimmicks here, so if we think you're mainly here to get views for your shit then we won't keep you around. Even if you're not new, advertising may still get you banned, so check with a mod first.

Piracy And Leaks

You won't be banned for warez/leaks unless:

  • You provide links
  • You ask for links
  • You hint at where to download
  • You openly declare or brag about it

Posting screenshots/videos of pirated/leaked content is fine.

Nudity and Sex

Posting of nudity and sex is fine - in the right context. For example:

  • Posting nudity in a thread about trains will get you banned.
  • Posting nudity in a thread about hot naked ladies won't get you banned.

Garry's Mod 'porn' and 'sexposes' are forbidden except in the GMF and OIFY.

Image Macros

Image macros are stupid and will get you banned - with the following exception

  • You won't get banned if you personally have made an image macro especially for that reply

What this is saying basically is don't post image macros from image macro sites. They're not funny.
This also applies to using short video clips as reaction replies.

Internet Memes

This isn't 4chan. Refrain from posting them here.

Image Posting

Posting large images won't get you banned and wherever possible it's encouraged to post your images in the thread rather than linking to them. There is no upper limit to the size of images you can post. Obviously if you're posting huge images with the aim of annoying or trolling other forum users you will get banned for it.

Reporting

If you see someone breaking the rules, use the report button ( ) to the left of their posts to report it. If you reply to tell them they're breaking the rules, you'll get banned too. Especially if you didn't report it.

Garry

Garry doesn't need to provide a legitimate reason to ban anyone. These are his forums.
If you're a dick to him he will ban you no matter how right or wrong you are.

Image/File Attachments

Facepunch Forums does not allow for direct file/image uploads. Here are some recommended online servcies you can use to host your images and files and link them on the forums:
  • Imgur - simple image hosting
  • FileSmelt - simple file hosting
